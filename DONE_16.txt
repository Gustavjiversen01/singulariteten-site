HOW IT BEHAVES
- Head tags in each language:
  - title, description, canonical, and hreflang for da, en and x-default;
  - Open Graph (article, locale da_DK / en_GB, the 1200x630 image with its size and alt text);
  - a Twitter summary_large_image card.
- Language:
  - the path decides it (/ Danish, /en/ English);
  - #da / #en and ?lang= still override it;
  - the DA / EN toggle changes the address to match, without reloading.
- Privacy:
  - no cookies, no localStorage or other browser storage, nothing loaded from other sites;
  - the check confirms it on all four views, so no cookie banner is needed;
  - the old website remembered the language choice in localStorage; the launch site uses the address instead.
- Analytics: off. ANALYTICS = False in final_k05/_build/launch.py is the one-line switch for Plausible
  (cookieless, EU).
- Newsletter: provider-agnostic, still not connected. NEWSLETTER in final_k05/_build/site.py has two modes
  with placeholders written out:
  - page mode for Substack opens Substack's signup page in a new tab;
  - form mode for MailerLite sends the address in the background with MailerLite's hidden fields.
  Both modes were tested locally with stand-in addresses.
